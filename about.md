<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

Passionate about the mountains and outdoor sports from an early age Perrine is there to help you progress at you own pace and in your own way.

Perrine has not only been a ski instructor since 2009, she also graduated top of her class nationally and competed on the international circuit for five years. She also teaches English in local schools, comes from a family of musicians, and enjoys sewing and crocheting in her spare time. She is truly multi-talented!

Whether you are new to skiing, or looking to improve and refine your skills, on or off-piste, discover the secret passages of the Evasion ski area, or wanting to try ski touring, Perrine is there to help you progress in safety and with a smile!

Fun, supportive and professional, Perrine is there to share her passion for the mountains!
</div>
